Double-click to add a note · drag to move (Shift = no snap, Alt/Option = copy) · drag edges to resize · double-click a note to delete · drag the empty grid to select · velocity lane at the bottom · drag in the ruler to set the loop. Touch: tap a note to select it and show its handles, drag a handle to resize (every selected note) · hold a note to select its chord · hold the empty grid, then drag to select · one finger on the empty grid scrolls · double-tap to add or delete · Fine / Alt / Shift in the toolbar stand in for the keys (tap = next gesture, double-tap = lock). Double-tap (double-click) a lane in the arrangement to edit its notes here, from that bar. Zoom: Ctrl/⌘-wheel or pinch sideways = time; Alt-wheel, pinch up / down (or on the keys at the left) or Keys −/+ = key size; two fingers scroll. The keys at the left play one note at a time — play chords on the keyboard below. Play: computer keys A–; (white) and W E T Y U O P (black), Z/X octave, C/V velocity. Click or touch the keyboard (lower on a key = louder); drag the tab on its top edge to make the keys taller or shorter, Key size for more or fewer keys; tap the tab (or pull it all the way down) to fold the keyboard away to its tab, and tap or pull it up to bring it back. Or plug in a MIDI controller (Chrome, Edge, Firefox — and the SYNTH app on iPad: USB, Bluetooth, or another MIDI app sending to it; put that app beside SYNTH in Split View) — each track listens on its MIDI channel. Studio: Space play/stop, R record, M metronome, Home to start, Tab CANVAS ↔ ROLL, Ctrl/⌘ Z undo. Keys go by position, so any layout works: if R, M or Q is a note key on yours, use the key where it sits on a US keyboard. Canvas: the selected track's instrument, its effects and its output sit on the canvas: drag empty space to pan, pinch (or Ctrl/⌘-wheel) to zoom, drag a module by its top bar, Delete / Backspace: delete the selected modules. The tab under the top row shows the track list (tap it, or pull it down); the tab at the left edge opens Sources, the instruments (tap it, or pull it out) — tap one to use it, or drag it onto the canvas (replaces the instrument) or the track list (a new track). Controls: drag up/down, Shift-drag for fine, scroll wheel, double-click to reset. App: the full-screen button (in the ≡ menu, where the browser has one) hides the browser’s bars; Install app (on iPad / iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen) puts SYNTH on your home screen or dock — it then opens full screen like an app and works offline after its first launch. On iPad the installed app is the better full screen: the browser’s keeps its own controls and may stop fast playing with a warning. On iPad and iPhone the installed app keeps its own projects, separate from the browser’s: Save a project in one (the app saves through the share sheet: Save to Files) and Open it in the other to move it across. Fonts: Inter and JetBrains Mono, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (the licence texts ship with SYNTH, in fonts/).

  

    
How it works. Every sound is generated in real time by a custom DSP engine running on the browser’s audio thread — no samples. Oscillators, filters, envelopes and effects are modelled from their circuits and computed in 64-bit floating point at up to 8× the output sample rate (at most 384 kHz internally), then band-limited back down with ~100 dB Kaiser half-band filters. Instrument and company names are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to describe the instruments being emulated; this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by them. The instrument drawings are original illustrations.
